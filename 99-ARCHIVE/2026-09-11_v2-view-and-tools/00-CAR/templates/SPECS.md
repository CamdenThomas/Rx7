# SPECS — the factory numbers

*Rev 2026-09-03 · owns: every hard number about this car that came from a document — {{count:specs}} rows in `data/specs.csv`, each citing its source in [`../01-REFERENCE/`](../01-REFERENCE/README.md) (`S-###`). Read this before looking anything up; add to it the session a new number is found.*

**How to read it.** `primary` = from a Mazda document. `secondary` = a vendor or club table that reproduces factory data. `unverified` = an enthusiast figure, kept only where nothing better exists — confirm on the car before relying on it. Where two documents disagree both rows are kept and the note says so. The 1982 car sits between the 1980 and 1985 manuals that were obtainable; a 1982-specific figure is marked in the note when one exists.
