# Research notes — 1982 RX-7 GS (FB, 12A, automatic) reference hunt

*2026-09-03. Companion to `01-REFERENCE/data/sources.csv` (34 sources, 12 obtained) and `00-CAR/data/specs.csv` (230 rows, 224 of them from Mazda documents).*

The strongest primary document is the one the owner already had: the 1982 factory wiring diagram (S-001). Its page 9 is a one-sheet schematic that names every fuse, its rating, its wire colour and the circuits it feeds, plus all four fusible links; pages 16, 20, 22 and 26 print the wattage of every bulb in the car. Every fuse and bulb row in the spec table comes from it. The only other 1982-specific find is the 1982 sales brochure (S-004), a Mazda scan giving the 100 hp / 105 lb-ft rating, the 45 Ah (55 Ah with automatic) battery, the 3-speed automatic ratios, curb weight and dimensions.

No 1981–83 workshop manual is openly hosted. The closest substitutes, all downloaded from foxed.ca into `01-REFERENCE/manuals/` (153 MB), are the complete 1985 workshop manual in 19 section PDFs (S-002), the 1980 manual's Technical Data section (S-003, the only source found for a factory torque table, wheel-nut torque and the 3N71B fluid capacity), the 1981–83 parts fiche (S-005, which gave the alternator, starter, coil and plug part numbers), and the 1981 dealer training manual (S-006, whose page 47 is a 1981 engine tune-up table that applies directly to 1982). Two 1985 files on that server, Technical Data and Wheels & Tires, are byte-damaged and were discarded. The 1982 owner's manual is paywalled; rx7club.com, automobile-catalog.com and mazdabg.com refused automated access and are listed as leads only.

To confirm on the car before they drive the electrical design: the alternator rating (Mazda's 1985 manual says 55 A for the 12A, the 1980 manual also says 55 A, an enthusiast chart says 50 A — read the stamp on the case); the battery tray size (factory JIS 50D20L/65D23L, retail lists Group 35); engine oil capacity (5.2 L for the 1981–82 front oil cooler versus 4.6 L from 1983); fuel pump pressure (two Mazda figures, 2.8–3.5 psi and 3.7–4.1 psi); the distributor pick-up coil (1050 Ω in 1981 versus 650 Ω in 1985) and ignition coil primary (1.35 Ω in 1985 versus 0.9 Ω in 1980); the caster spec; and the 4x110 bolt pattern, ET20 offset and 26 psi tyre pressure, which rest on tertiary or 1980 sources. The redline was not found in any Mazda document and was deliberately left out.
