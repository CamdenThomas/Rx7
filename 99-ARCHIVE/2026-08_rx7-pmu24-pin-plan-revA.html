§1

Two-tier architecture

The 39-pin is a device connector, not a harness connector — it stays on the panel and gets opened exactly once. Its wires travel six to eighteen inches to a busbar and to regional bulkhead connectors on the panel edge. Every harness segment plugs into its own region. Expansion means uncapping a reserved wire at a bulkhead, never re-terminating the 39-pin.

Terminate all 39 cavities now. Reserved channels get real wire run to a capped spare bundle at the relevant bulkhead. A half-pinned connector means pulling the PMU and opening a sealed housing later — which is the exact thing this architecture exists to prevent.

§5

Build rules

  1. Terminate all 39 cavitiesReserved channels get real wire, routed to the correct bulkhead and capped there. The 39-pin is assembled once.
  2. Wire class reads as colorBase color says which channel class it came from — RED for 25 A, ORN for 15 A, VIO for 7 A, GRY for inputs. Tracer says which circuit. You can identify a wire's origin without a diagram.
  3. Grounds never cross a bulkheadEach zone gets a local star node. The only ground on the panel is pin 25, and it is the shortest, heaviest wire in the car — it carries flyback return for every inductive load on the device.
  4. Wipers only on O8It is the one channel with motor braking. Nothing else goes there and wipers go nowhere else.
  5. The worst inductive loads on O1 and O16Only these two carry integrated high-power flyback diodes. Motor bus and blower.
  6. Switch inputs go to groundA1–A8 read 0–5 V. Use the internal 10 kΩ pull-up and switch to ground. Only A9–A16 read 12 V directly, which is why key and headlight switch live there.
  7. Ladder every multi-position switch nowRetrofitting a resistor ladder onto a switch already wired point-to-point is a re-pin. Wiper stalk, headlight switch, key, turn stalk, pop-up limits, door pins — all ladders from day one.
  8. Keep a true-constant bus off the PMUThe device sleeps. Head unit clock and the diagnostic port feed straight off the busbar through their own fuses. Anything that must survive a PMU shutdown does not belong on an output.
  9. Set soft fuses from measured currentEnable one output at a time on the bench, read live current in the client, then set the limit. Never from a guess, never from the estimates in this document.
  10. Full cavity count, alwaysBuy every housing at its full position count and fill unused cavities with a capped wire — not a sealing plug. Cheap now, a teardown later.
