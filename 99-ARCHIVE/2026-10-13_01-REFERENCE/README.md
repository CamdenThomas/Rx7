# The reference's record (archived 2026-10-13, D-519, 06-intake T3)

`01-REFERENCE` was an area from the start: the manuals, the factory circuits, the sources and
the found models, with tables of its own. D-519 made it an intake, not a home. Its tables went
to the car on 2026-10-13 (06-intake T1): `sources`, `catalogue`, `cad`, `suppliers`,
`connector_dims` and `circuits` into `00-CAR`, ids unchanged, and `blend` into `02-PROJECTS/05-cad`.
What is left here is the shell it kept until the end: `_project`, `_tables`, `_schema`, an
empty `blocks`, and `retired`, whose one row (`REF.01`, replaced by block 06.03) is also in
`06-intake`'s. It stays so the `REF` block numbers are never handed out again. The folder
itself was renamed `01-INTAKE` the same day and is no longer an area.
