**D-260 — The oil-pressure node is excited by the PMU, not the ICU: a 100 Ω 1 W pull-up from pin 15 (+5 V) to the node, on the dash node beside the two bias resistors. A7 keeps its 1 MΩ pull-down; the ICU reads the same node with no pull-up of its own.** 2026-09-07 — the one exception to D-258's "the ICU excites the senders", forced by D-251: the fuel-pump gate reads this node, and a gate whose excitation came from the ICU would depend on the ICU — reboot the module and the pump stops five seconds later. With the PMU exciting it, A7 reads a clean DC node whatever the ICU is doing; the ICU is the second reader and drives the old gauge from what it reads. An open sender wire reads full scale and a short reads zero, both outside any real sender value, so both are FAULT windows and fail open (D-249); the old gauge's pulsing regulator is gone from the node, so the reading is steadier than it was. Half a watt at a shorted sender; `P114` is the two resistors (one fitted, one spare). *Supersedes* D-249's reading method ("the factory gauge still drives the sender, A7 observes the node") and D-251's "three readers" clause — two readers now, the PMU and the ICU; the gauge is downstream of the ICU.
