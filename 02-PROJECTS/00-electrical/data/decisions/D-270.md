**D-270 — The ICU lives in a protected enclosure with its connector halves built into the wall: two Deutsch DT13 flanged PCB receptacles (6-way power / bus, 12-way sensors) on the board, sealed through one face; the display ribbon and the page button through grommets in the opposite face; an RF-transparent wall in front of the radio's antenna. The harness side of `DP-ICU-A` / `DP-ICU-B` becomes a DT06 plug on a 150 mm tail from the dash post, so the ICU plugs in like any bought module. The enclosure is 3D-printed in ASA or PETG (never PLA — a dash reaches 70 °C in the sun) around the board, with printed bosses for the DT13 flanges and a gasket groove in the lid; a polycarbonate Hammond 1554-series box is the fallback if printing disappoints.** Camden's call, 2026-09-08, answering `Q-126`: "keep the ICU in a protected container … ideally the connectors are embedded and the harness just plugs in like any other bought part" — and his question, one connector like the PMU or two: **two**, because the whole harness is already tooled for DT (a single 18-way would mean an HD30 / DRC family, new contacts, new tools, a bigger flange), because the 6-way is the module pattern every drop shares (D-252) and the 12-way keeps sensors on their own housing, and because "out" is only the display — CAN is on the 6-way both ways, so nothing else leaves the box. Consequences: `DP-ICU-A` / `DP-ICU-B` box_side become DT13-06PA / DT13-12PA (P122); the two DT04 receptacle kits leave the Deutsch count (P099, P115 re-drawn) and the two post clips with them (P030 → 21); `V-073`'s edge rule (D-265) is the enclosure's face rule. Printing is at CSU's makerspace if it has one, else any print service — the file is ours either way.
