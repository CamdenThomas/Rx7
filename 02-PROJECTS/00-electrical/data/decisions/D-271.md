**D-271 — Reserves live at the post, never in the engine bay. The engine leg carries only what the 12A on the mounts needs plus the CAN2 terminator drop; every future-engine provision is pinned on the PMU side of the L1 receptacles and sealed on the leg side. A new cavity state, RESERVED, says exactly that: `L1-P 2` / `L1-P 3` (O13 / O14, 12 AWG from the pins to the receptacle cavities), `L1-S2 9` (the spare block-B position wired to its cavity, no fuse) and `L1-S2 11` (wired across the node to the ICU's spare input `DP-ICU-B 9`) are RESERVED; `L1-S2 4 · 5 · 6` become sealing plugs — a sensor spare with no destination at the post is pinned at the swap, when it has one. The same rule for the chassis legs: a branch with no device ends at the last connector of the main leg, not at a capped wire near where a device might one day be (`Q-132` applies it to the three existing stubs).** Camden's call, 2026-09-08, answering `Q-127` in his own words: "wire the PMU, ICU and DCU all in one go, supporting any engine … the chassis legs go fully wired, but branches with no end are just a connector and the main leg; the engine leg is the unique one — completely clean for the current 12A, connecting to the PMU side via a connector with many unpinned spots, so the PMU side can be fully pinned for as many engines and transmissions as possible, but I don't have to wire them past the PMU / fuses / relays." This is D-211(d) made precise: the interface to any future engine is the L1 receptacles at the dash post, pinned; the leg itself is disposable. What leaves the carts: about 110 ft of wire and eight leg-side contacts; what joins: one sealing plug per RESERVED cavity on the leg side (the size-12 and size-16 plug lines are re-counted). `tools/rx7.py` learned the state: the cut list prints a RESERVED cavity as "no leg conductor", the sealing-plug counts take one half per RESERVED cavity, and a pin's destinations read "reserved at the post". *Supersedes* D-007's "capped at the bulkhead" for O13 / O14 and the three sensor spares, and D-250's leg-side conductors for `L1-S2 9` and `11`; D-252's tunnel pair `L4-S 3 / 4` is untouched — it is a chassis leg and now has a device (`Q-309`).
