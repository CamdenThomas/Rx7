**D-264 — The 12A's electrical sensors are the three senders and nothing else. The cut B-12 water-temperature switch stays in the thermostat housing as the port's plug, unconnected; the oil level float switch (C-06) is not wired and the CLU pigtail stays at twelve lines; there is no oil-temperature sender on the 12A and no conductor is run for one — `L1-S2 10` and `DP-ICU-B 7` become sealing plugs, `N65` and `P112` leave the record, and the ICU keeps the IC07 footprint for the swap.** Camden's three answers, 2026-09-07: `Q-124` "verified unneeded — leave it as the plug, unconnected"; `Q-125` "no need for the added warning light — I check the oil every time I get gas"; `Q-308` "no oil-temp sensor while the 12A is there; prepare for after the swap, but run no unneeded wire to the engine bay". The last restates the engine-bay rule (D-211(d)) and it wins over D-250's oil-temperature conductor: the leg is rebuilt at the swap anyway, so a wire run now for a sender that will arrive with a different engine serves nothing. What the swap needs is at the dash node — a cavity (`DP-ICU-B 7`, plugged) and a channel (IC07, footprint) — and both stay. Whether the *rest* of L1's reserves follow is `Q-127`. *Supersedes* D-250's oil-temperature conductor (its sensor supply `L1-S2 9` and spare `L1-S2 11` stand until Q-127) and the A-010 clause that left the level senders as a future item — they are out.
