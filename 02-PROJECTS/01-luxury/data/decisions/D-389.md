**The ruling.** Each proposed pick gets its own block, built from its `picks` row:
- **Ask:** buy this product, at this price, for this part?
- **Why:** what the part has to be, then what meets it, why this one, its drawbacks, what to confirm before buying, the confidence, and where to buy it.
- **Options:** (a) accept; (b) veto, with the runner-up that goes up next and its drawback.
- **Recommend:** the plain advice, or the pick's own `recommend` cell where the plain advice is not the honest one (the headlamps, the blower controller).
- **Stops:** only that part's line on the cart.

His answer goes on the `**SOLVE:**` line directly under the suggestion. `BLOCKS.md` stays the one file he types in (R3).
