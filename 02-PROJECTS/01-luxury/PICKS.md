### PK021 · LP53 LED bulbs - front exterior — Diode Dynamics DD0015P (pair)
**Suggest** Diode Dynamics 1157 XP80 amber, front turn and park (LA02) (Diode Dynamics DD0015P (pair)) · $100 · round 2
**It has to be** Front turn (amber), front park, front side markers (amber) - one per lamps row whose part is LP53
**What meets it** 1157 (BAY15d) as the 1982 diagram shows; 510 lm, 0.63 A at 13.2 V; nonpolar; sold for the 1979-1991 RX-7; claimed dual-brightness for park and turn
**Why this one** The brightest published amber 1157 - a turn signal replacing a 27 W bulb needs the output, and Diode Dynamics is the maker with published numbers
**Drawbacks** Its own page does not describe the park (low) mode, and no dimensions are published - it is the bulkier one, so it may not fit the front lamp housing. Diode Dynamics' resistor kit is not needed (the PMU makes the flasher)
**Confirm before buying** That it fits inside the front combo lamp housing; the park brightness
**Confidence** medium
**Where** Diode Dynamics — <https://www.diodedynamics.com/front-turn-signal-leds-for-1979-1991-mazda-rx-7-pair.html>
**If you say no** next up is Diode Dynamics 1157 HP48 amber (Diode Dynamics DD0013P (pair)), $50: Half the price and smaller - more likely to fit. Its drawback: Half the light of the XP80. Unless your reason rules it out too.
**Recommend** Yes if it fits the housing; if you would rather not risk the fit, say no and the smaller HP48 goes up
