Overhaul CAD Pass

The overhaul's plan is done and in the record (D-596 to D-612). This page is the brief for the next pass, which is CAD: one section per box, saying which work row it is, what it reads from the record, what to draw, what must hold, and how it is checked. Every number comes from the record. Where this page and the record disagree, the record is right, and a drawing that needs a pin or a part changed changes the record first.

An orange tag ? marks a figure guessed for the drawing. Nothing in the car has been measured, and the space is assumed until it is (D-603, R11).

How every box is drawn

Read the record, never this page

A box's contents are its rows. Each section below names the queries. For example:

rx7.py sql 02-PROJECTS/01-electrical "select * from housings where box='PMU-BOX'"

rx7.py sql 02-PROJECTS/01-electrical "select * from cavities where housing='L1-E1'"

The box's own row is in boxes: where it sits, what it holds, how it joins the harness, and its rulings.

Files go where parts.py says

Each box has a part folder: pmu/, display-unit/, control-unit/ (with control-unit-remote/), bin-box/, bay-junction/ and amp-dock/, each with cad, data and diagrams. A new board or mock-up is added to tools/parts.py (BOARDS, ASSEMBLIES) before its first file, and every tool finds it from there.

A mock-up's numbers go in one table, <part>/data/<name>_params.py, built by tools/mockup.py. A board is a KiCad project in cad/<board>/, held to the record by tools/check.py.

Record first

If a drawing moves a cavity or changes a part, the record changes in the same pass, through rx7.py, and rx7.py check stays clean. Then rx7.py diagrams runs if a housing, cavity, device or route changed. A box is done when its boxes.state reads drawn, and the outdated banner leaves its READMEs.

Nothing measured is final

The space under the dash (C1, M-1), the cargo bin (M-4) and the binnacle are assumed. Every envelope is a parameter marked confirm, so the mock-ups are rebuilt from the numbers once he measures (D-603, R11).

The plan sheets

Drawn from the record by rx7.py diagrams, so each one shows the plan as the tables hold it now and is redrawn whenever they change (D-613). The CAD's own drawings carry an orange OUTDATED banner until their row redraws them.

PMU box · Y69

Reads

  • boxes PMU-BOX
  • housings and cavities where box = PMU-BOX: L1-E1, L1-E2, L1-E3, L2-F1, L4-R1, and the hard-wired L3-COL, L3-DASH, L3-HU
  • node_conductors, the box's inside connection list, every row
  • fuses and relays where box = PMU-BOX
  • pins, wake_stages, loads, logic, ladders

Draw

  • The board. Eight smart high-side switches (P239) where K1–K4, K11, K12, K14 and K15 were. The two window H-bridges (P240, K5–K8). K13 in a socket. The wake circuit as wake_stages has it. The A15 / A16 pull-ups, the oil-pressure pull-up and A7's tap. The pop-up diodes (N09), the main-feed TVS (P136), the CAN2 splice and the ground star. The series ladder resistors whose switch has its own wire (D-610).
  • The fuse banks. Big fuses F30, F31, F32, F35 and F36 at the feed stud (P241). Sixteen mini blades under the lid (P242), with the map inside the lid.
  • The enclosure (P243). The PMU-24 as one outside wall. The five HDP20 jam-nut receptacles face down or sideways (the water rule), with L1-E2 / L1-E3 and L2-F1 / L4-R1 keyed apart. Glands for the hard-wired bundles. The 2 AWG feed stud and one heavy ground lead out to the chassis point (D-603). Room for the EMU PRO on its pigtails (D-601).
  • The heat budget. Each switch's and bridge's dissipation at loads' design currents, plus the PMU's own. It decides between the two shapes: stacked, about 112 × 175 × 55 ?, or flat, about 112 × 250 × 45 ?.

Must hold

  • Each switch does exactly what its relay did. High-side ones (K11 on O22, K14 / K15 on O15) take a PMU output. Low-side ones (K3 / K4 from the CU, K12 from the stalk's WASH contact, K1 / K2 through each wink's NC pole) take a ground. The pop-ups are two switches a side, interlocked, with "up" following the lamps while the wink pole is closed. A switch that fails on must still be stopped by its fuse.
  • F24 is fed from F3's output, so a light-side short blows only F24 (D-599).
  • Every dash return lands on the star (D-603). CAN2 runs PMU box → DU → CU, with the 120 Ω at the CU end (N74).
  • L1-E1 1–16 serve today's engine leg. The box sides of L1-E2 / L1-E3 and of L1-E1 17–29 are wired from day one to where the ECU's pigtails will land; the ECU end waits on its pinout (block APP.05).
  • The roadside recovery (recovery, D-322) still works with switches under a lid. Re-check it.

Checked by, and open

  • ERC and DRC at zero. tools/check.py --board pmu-board (a new board in parts.py) agrees with node_conductors and the cavities. The mock-up's self-test passes.
  • The switches and the pop-up interlock simulated like the wake decks (sim/). The wake pulse capacitor is 4.7 µF X7R if ceramic.
  • Open: which insert numbers are #12, #16 and #20 (TE C-0425-014-2400 rev H) ?; the key codes; the switch part per channel; L1-E1 4's source in the box (the A/C clutch, LW32).

  • Open: whether the radar receiver fits (luxury Z-002); the DRC part numbers from the maker's drawing.

Bin box · Y73

Reads and draws

  • boxes BIN-BOX; fuses where box = BIN-BOX; cables C01–C05, C11–C14; backbone
  • A mock-up around the Ionic S9 and its tray, held down the way the battery is (D-063). The master disconnect's knob comes through the lid. Inside: the Class-T, the 2151 dual MRBF (the starter's 250 A and F34), F38 and the negative bus.
  • Four bolted studs under the lid: the PMU feed C03, the starter feed C04, the cranking return C11 and the chassis bond C05. The two booted battery leads go onto the brass terminals (P095). The dock's two leads are C13 and C14.

Must hold

  • The box sets on beside the battery and its terminals tighten; removal is the reverse.
  • Every exposed lug is booted. The disconnect is on the positive side (D-319).
  • Open: the cargo bin's space (M-4, V-088) ?.

Bay junction · Y74

Reads and draws

  • boxes BAY-JCT; fuses F17, F18; relays K9; cables C04, C07–C09, C11, C15
  • One plate on the body where the starter feed arrives. It carries the covered positive jump post, the ground post bonded to the block, the MIDI block (F17, F18) and K9's socket. C15 is a short 1/0 lead to the starter's B+ stud, and at the LS swap only that lead changes.

Must hold

  • B4 (D-609): use the S9's own emergency-start reserve first; jump through the post at 14.6 V at most; never jump another car from it until Ionic says so; ask Ionic the charge-current limit.
  • C15's length is measured. Today it is a confirm.

Amplifier dock · Y75

Reads and draws

  • boxes AMP-DOCK; cavities L4-AMP 1–29; devices DV60, DV66; luxury LP56, LP57
  • The upmix connector board (P247), bolted to the amplifier, with L4-AMP's receptacle and the digital link inside. It docks against the bin box with two bolted leads.
  • The subs are on #12. The other speakers, the head unit's line-level pair with its drain on its own cavity, and the remote are on #16.

Must hold

  • The amplifier's fins stay in open air, away from the battery.
  • It waits on the amplifier's pick (luxury X-012).

After the boxes

Y76 · The build track

New rows build and bench-test each box whole before it goes in the car: continuity, isolation, then power through a current limit with every output exercised. They replace E13 to E18. The rows gated on Y76 (E6, E7, E9 to E11, E20 to E24, E26, E36, E41) are rewritten with their briefs and steps, every figure from a row (D-533).

Y77 · The app's 3D

tools/export_glb.py exports the boxes as drawn, and the scene drops the dash post and the old unit outlines.

Y78 · Break points

With every wire placed, choose where the dash's runs split for installing and servicing. The nose at the core support, the hatch at its hinge and the doors at the A-pillar are the natural ones.
