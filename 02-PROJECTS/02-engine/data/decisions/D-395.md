**The plan: 63 new rows, each gated on what it truly waits for.**
- **-01 Design:** DS01–DS11. They cover the accessory drive, the FB-clearance oil pan, headers, cooling against O14's 25 A, the returnless fuel system, the clutch pedal and hydraulics (the car is an automatic today), mounts and crossmember, the rear axle (D-113), springs, and a derived budget. DS11 is his design freeze. The rows already in the project join this stage: K1 the ECU, K2 emissions, K3 the cavity plan, J3 and J4 the tunnel, H-001 the alternator.
- **00 Buy and retrieve:** BR01–BR06. They cover stands and tools, the yard pull (with the list of what to take), the CD009 after the tunnel is measured and the design frozen, the adapter kit and clutch, the rebuild parts from the breakdown's findings, and the car-prep parts. J1, J2 and J5 join this stage.
- **01 Breakdown:** BD01–BD08. They record the numbers, then read the fluids, strip and label, take the heads off where the AFM delete needs it, and inspect the pan and bearings. The CD009 is checked on its stand. The breakdown ends with his ruling on the rebuild scope, and K4 (read the real pin letters) joins it.
- **02 Polish:** PO01–PO04. Degrease with every opening plugged, derust the steel, and mask every machined face before paint.
- **03 Paint:** PA01–PA03. The colours are his call, because the engine and the bay are seen together. Then high-temperature enamel, and the small parts.
- **04 Rebuild:** RB01–RB08. The AFM delete (LH6 / LC9 only), timing and oil pump, heads on new single-use bolts, rear main and the swap pan, water pump, sensors and injectors, gaskets, the adapter, flywheel, clutch and CD009, then pre-lube.
- **05 Car prep:** CP01–CP10. The 12A and the automatic out (the old leg is cut here, D-211), then:
  - a clean bay (D-325), tunnel work and mounts;
  - the pedal box, fuel, cooling, axle and driveshaft;
  - the new engine leg and the ECU at the dash;
  - springs.
- **06 Install:** IN01–IN06. In the car, plumbing, electrical, fluids, then the checks before the first start: oil pressure, fuel pressure, crank and cam signals. Then the first start.
- **07 Fine-tune:** TU01–TU07. The base tune, then CAN integration: the ICU and the PMU's rpm terms (D-183), and the soft fuses set from measured currents. Then road tests with a charging check, a dyno tune, alignment, and emissions and registration. Last, the as-built goes into 00-CAR (CLAUDE.md §6.6).

**Rules the plan keeps.**
- **Nothing past the engine is bought before the design freeze (DS11).** It is the same discipline as the electrical project.
- **The breakdown's measurements set the rebuild scope, not the plan (R11).** Worn bores or bad bearings turn a reseal into a rebuild.
- **The donor harness is kept intact** as the reference for the new leg's pin letters.
