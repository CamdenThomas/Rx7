**D-249 — A7 leaves the fuel node and reads the oil-pressure node; the fuel pump is gated on it.** Camden's call, 2026-09-07, answering `Q-107` with (a). All ten PMU analog inputs were allocated, so the gate cost one of them, and fuel level was the right one to give up: it protects nothing, the factory gauge shows it anyway, and it comes back on CAN2 from the ICU (D-251). A7's tap moves from the fuel node to the oil-pressure node `N47` (`L1-S1 4 → DP-CLU 6`; `N45` re-drawn) — one wire moved at the dash node, no cavity, no cart line. The reading method is unchanged: the factory gauge still drives the sender, A7 observes the node through the 1 MΩ pull-down, and the threshold `OIL_MIN` is read in the car at commissioning (D-142), never entered from a datasheet. The `FUEL_PUMP` expression carries three conditions that are not optional — unconditional during START, a 3 s prime window from key-on so the float bowl fills, and a latch that clears only after 5 s of continuous zero or when the key leaves RUN — because a sender tap is a less trustworthy signal than a pressure switch, and this must never become a pump that cuts on a glitch at 60 mph; a FAULT reading (open or shorted wire) counts as pressure present and logs. The inertia switch (Q-107 option b) is not fitted; it can go in series at the rear at any time. *Supersedes* D-197 — the measured fuel curve it recorded (6 Ω full · 31.5 Ω mid · 80 Ω empty, nonlinear, a three-point lookup trued in the car) now belongs to the ICU's fuel input on `DP-ICU-B 3` — and D-215's A7 clause; the fuel-pump half of D-183's interim rule is replaced by the gate above, and its rpm term, when CAN brings one, obeys D-251.
