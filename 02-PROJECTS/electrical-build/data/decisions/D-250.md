**D-250 — The engine leg carries a switched sensor supply and two signal spares for the ICU's Phase-2 sensors: `L1-S2 9` (+12 V, RED), `L1-S2 10` (oil temperature, GRY) and `L1-S2 11` (spare, GRY), capped 12 in past the grommet like the swap reserves.** Camden's call, 2026-09-07, answering `Q-111` with (b) — and (a) with it: the three original senders are replaced now with factory-spec resistive parts, fitted *before* the in-car calibration reads, and the tank sender's curve is re-measured after the swap, not before. A 3-wire transducer or a thermistor with a published curve wants a regulated supply, a signal and a ground where this design runs one wire; the cavities were free but the supply was not, and adding it later means the firewall grommet, the leg and the dash. No +5 V goes down the leg (D-215 stands): the supply is switched 12 V from a spare block-B fuse position, fitted only when a sensor arrives, regulated at the sender or the dash node, and the same position also feeds the road-speed sensor (`L4-S 4`, D-252). At the dash node the two signals land on `DP-ICU-B 7` and `9` (`N65`, `N67`); the supply is capped at the node (`N68`).
