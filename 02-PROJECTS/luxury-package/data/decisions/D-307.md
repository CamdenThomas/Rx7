**D-307 — Blower speed is the DCU's command and the PMU's PWM: the DCU sends a duty on `0x300` byte 1, the PMU applies it to O16, and the motor is a 2-wire brushed blower (Four Seasons 35483 class, motor only — the squirrel cage transfers) that this project buys and plugs into `L3-BLW` at the HVAC case; no resistor pack, no speed switch, no blower power stage on the DCU carrier.** Camden's call, 2026-09-07 (electrical `Q-112` → D-253). The DCU's blower output is a CAN field, not a FET — `V-083`'s comfort FETs serve seats, mirror heat and nozzles only. *Fallback, decided at bring-up:* if the PMU's PWM frequency makes the blower hum at part speed, a low-side final stage (an OEM-style blower regulator or a ≥ 20 kHz PWM module, mounted in the resistor pack's hole for airflow) inserts at `L3-BLW` as a plug-in adapter and takes its command from the DCU on a local pigtail — the harness does not change either way. On a lost `0x300` the PMU holds a default duty; the timeout row says so. Two more things the DCU owns from the same session: **outside-air temperature** arrives on `DP-DCU 2` (electrical D-256) and is published in `0x300` byte 5 beside cabin temperature, for the cluster to display — there is no automatic climate control and none is planned; and the **heated-nozzle / park-de-icer feed** is run on `L2-S 6`, conditional on `Q-303`. Mirror adjustment has its conductors (`L4-S2`, electrical D-255); whether a mechanical switch in the panel or the DCU's bridges drive them is this project's choice when the panel is designed — D-305's feature set (adjust and heat only) is unchanged.
