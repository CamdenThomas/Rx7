# CONNECTOR SCHEDULE — housings, series and the dash post

*Rev 2026-08-31 · owns: housing part numbers, series selection, wedgelocks and accessories. Cavity assignments are [`PIN-MAP.md`](PIN-MAP.md)'s.*

Every connector in the car, coded. Power and signal are deliberately split into
separate housings wherever the conductor classes differ enough to matter
(D-032). Legs are cut by removal boundary, not connector convenience (D-029);
the seven-region C1–C7 scheme this replaced is in `99-ARCHIVE`.

## 1 · Naming convention

```
L<leg>-<class><index>         leg-side connector
DP-<code>                     dash post, box side
D1 / D2                       door connectors at the sill
```

| Class | Meaning | Conductor |
|---|---|---|
| **P** | Power, heavy | 12 AWG, 25 A |
| **M** | Power, medium | 14–16 AWG, 7–15 A |
| **S** | Signal | 16 AWG, <1 A |

## 2 · Why power and signal are split

1. **Terminal size.** A 12 AWG leg needs a size-12 contact; a signal needs
   size-16. Mixed housings cost cavity efficiency and stock two crimp tools'
   worth of parts per connector.
2. **Noise.** The tach pickup and the ladder references would share a bundle
   with pop-up motor legs if they shared a housing. Separate housings let the
   bundles route apart.
3. **Service.** Unplugging the signal half to diagnose a switch shouldn't
   require breaking a 25 A motor circuit.

## 3 · Series selection — final (D-070)

| Series | Contact | Wire | Amps | Used for |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Deutsch DTP** | Size 12 | 10–14 AWG seal | 25 A | All `P` connectors |
| **Deutsch DT** | Size 16 | 14–20 AWG seal | 13 A | All `M` and `S` connectors, D1/D2, DP-ICU, DP-DCU |
| **Deutsch DTM** | Size 20 | 14–22 AWG seal | 7.5 A | DP-DIAG and DP-KEY only |

**No AMPSEAL anywhere.** The whole car is two contact sizes, two crimp dies,
one catalogue. DTP is only made in 2-way and 4-way (D-115), which is why L2
carries two DTP shells.

Leg side is always the **socket** (`06-xS`), box side always the **pin**
(`04-xP`), so a leg can never be plugged into the wrong half.

## 5 · The dash post — the unified junction

Everything the legs plug into, plus what the box itself needs.

| Code | What | Type | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **DP-BAT** | Main +12 V in | M8 stud + ring lug | 2 AWG from the Class-T (D-091) |
| **DP-GND** | Ground bus | Bus bar + ring lugs | Panel ground; pin 25 lands here on 10 AWG, ≤6 in |
| **DP-PMU** | PMU device connector | Sicma 39-pos | **Sealed once, never reopened** (D-004) |
| **DP-DIAG** | Diagnostic / CAN1 port | DTM04-4P | Glovebox. 120 Ω here and at the PMU |
| **DP-KEY** | Control-panel drop — **device deferred** (D-210) | DTM04-4P | CAN2 + switched power + ground; wired and capped for the custom A/C panel |
| **DP-ICU** | Instrument cluster unit | DT04-12P | Power, CAN2, dimming reference, six sensors + brake fluid (D-083) |
| **DP-DCU** | Dash control unit | DT04-6P | Power, CAN2 (D-083) |
| **DP-L1-P … DP-L4-S** | Leg receptacles ×14 | see §4 | Bracket-mounted on the panel edge with `1027-003-1200` clips |

The dash post is a location, not a product. The ~450 mm of receptacle
frontage it needs is the number [`PANEL-LAYOUT.md`](../01-DESIGN/PANEL-LAYOUT.md) checks against the dash
cavity (T-007).

## 6 · Verified from the TE catalogue (D-114 … D-117)

`TS-ICT-T-C-CAT-2018.pdf` in `01-REFERENCE`, DT family pages 109–132.

| Contact size | Series | Standard seal | Our wire |
|---|---|---|---|
| 20 | DTM | 14–22 AWG | 16 AWG drops — fine |
| **16** | **DT** | **14–20 AWG** | **16 AWG — comfortable** |
| **12** | **DTP** | **10–14 AWG** | **12 AWG — comfortable** |

**One tight spot:** the door window-motor legs are 14 AWG in a `DT06-08S`
(D-092). Size 16 seals 14–20 AWG, so 14 AWG is at the very top of the range —
it fits, with no margin above. They are provisioned and capped for now
(D-131), so nothing is at stake until windows arrive.

**`-C015` reduced-diameter seals** exist for thin-insulation wire. Buy
standard first; if 16 AWG GXL seals loosely on the bench, C015 is the answer
rather than switching wire (D-117).

spare.

## 8 · Housekeeping rules

- Every housing bought at **full cavity count**; unused positions get a capped
  wire, not a sealing plug (D-022).
- **Order 20 % spare contacts and wedgelocks** of every size. You will ruin
  some.
- Label both halves of every connector with its code before it leaves the
  bench.
- A leg comes out as a unit; its housings sit together on the post
  ([`PANEL-LAYOUT.md`](../01-DESIGN/PANEL-LAYOUT.md) principle 5).
