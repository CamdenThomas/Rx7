### Maintenance philosophy

The July 2026 brake work was **deliberately early**. Parts were replaced before
they were worn out, not after they failed.

That's worth recording because it explains the baseline: **assume brake and
bearing components are near-new, not near-end-of-life.** It also means intervals
on this car are set by choice rather than by symptom, so future planning should
work from mileage since replacement, not from "is it still working."
