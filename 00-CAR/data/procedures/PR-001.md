The system still holds charge (it still cools a little — K-015), and venting refrigerant is illegal. Have a shop recover it **before** anything on the circuit is opened. Then pull the compressor, bracket, belt, condenser, receiver/drier and lines; leave the blower, heater core, HVAC case, ducts and doors alone; box the hardware rather than scrapping it — FB A/C parts are scarce and cooling returns with the new engine. The compressor is on its own belt, so nothing else on the crank is disturbed.
